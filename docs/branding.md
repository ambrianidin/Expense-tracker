## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished square app icon for Savena, an Indonesian personal budget and savings planner. A bold, friendly custom capital S monogram formed from a single smooth flowing ribbon, suggesting steady saving. Solid violet #6a5ceb rounded-square tile, warm white S, one small restrained peach #f08a5d coin-like circular accent near the upper right of the S. Clean flat vector-like geometry, very thick simple shapes recognizable at 16px, strong contrast, optically centered. Tile fills 92% of square canvas, transparent exterior corners. No words, no extra letters, no currency signs, no piggy bank, no charts, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no presentation board. One icon only, front view.
